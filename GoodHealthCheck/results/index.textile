h2. Description of errors for EB

Dead pedestal  (DP)

* Gain 1 : MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.2
* Gain 6 : MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.4
* Gain 12: MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.5

 Bad pedestal   (BP)

* abs(MEAN - 220) >= 50 and MEAN > 1

 Large RMS      (LR)

* Gain 1 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 6 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 12: (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)

 Very Large RMS (VLR)

* Gain 1 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 6 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 12: (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)

h2. Description of errors for EE

Dead pedestal  (DP)

* Gain 1 : MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.2
* Gain 6 : MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.4
* Gain 12: MEAN <= 1 or RMS <= 0.5

 Bad pedestal   (BP)

* abs(MEAN - 260) >= 90 and MEAN > 1

 Large RMS      (LR)

* Gain 1 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 6 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 12: (not DP) and (RMS >= 5 and RMS < 10 and MEAN > 1)

 Very Large RMS (VLR)

* Gain 1 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 6 : (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)
* Gain 12: (not DP) and (RMS >= 10 and MEAN > 1)

h2. Description of Test Pulse errors

-----------EB------------

Dead TestPulse          (DTP)

* MEAN = 0

 Low TestPulse amplitude (STP)

* AVG = average mean for each subdetector (EB, EE)
* MEAN > 0 and MEAN < 0.5 * AVG

 Large TP amplitude      (LTP)

* MEAN > 1.5 * AVG

-----------EE------------

 Dead TestPulse          (DTP)

* MEAN = 0

 Low TestPulse amplitude (STP)

* AVG = average mean for each subdetector (EB, EE)
* MEAN > 0 and MEAN < 0.5 * AVG

 Large TP amplitude      (LTP)

* MEAN > 1.5 * AVG

h2. Description of Laser Pulse errors:

-----------EB------------

* DLAMPL: MEAN <= 5
* SLAMPL: MEAN > 0 and MEAN < AVG * 0.1         # AVG per subdetector
* LLERRO: MEAN > AVG * 0.1 and RMS / MEAN > 0.1 # AVG per subdetector
* DLAMPL_OVERPN: MEAN_OVER_PN <= 0.2
* SLAMPL_OVERPN: MEAN_OVER_PN > 0 and MEAN_OVER_PN < AVG * 0.1         # AVG per subdetector

-----------EE------------

* DLAMPL: MEAN <= 5
* SLAMPL: MEAN > 0 and MEAN < AVG * 0.005         # AVG per subdetector
* LLERRO: MEAN > AVG * 0.1 and RMS / MEAN > 0.1 # AVG per subdetector
* DLAMPL_OVERPN: MEAN_OVER_PN <= 0.001
* SLAMPL_OVERPN: MEAN_OVER_PN > 0 and MEAN_OVER_PN < AVG * 0.0005         # AVG per subdetector
